# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** May 28, 2026  
**Effective Date:** May 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ambimed Healthcare ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when users access or use the caregiver and home nurse booking application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Services"). This policy is intended to help users understand what data is collected, why it is collected, how it is used, and what choices and rights are available. [blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-mena/product-updates/android-chrome-and-play/google-play-safety/)

By creating an account, accessing, or using the Services, users acknowledge that they have read and understood this Privacy Policy. [onesignal](https://onesignal.com/blog/google-play-data-safety-compliance/)

## Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to personal data collected through the mobile application, any associated website, customer support interactions, and related booking or account management services offered by the Company. [blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-mena/product-updates/android-chrome-and-play/google-play-safety/)

This policy should be read together with any in-app disclosures and permission prompts presented at the time data is collected, especially where sensitive or device-level permissions are requested. [iubenda](https://www.iubenda.com/en/blog/fix-make-sure-apps-fulfill-prominent-disclosure-requirements/)

## Information Collected

The Services may collect information that users provide directly, information collected automatically through the app or device, and information generated through use of the Services. [onesignal](https://onesignal.com/blog/google-play-data-safety-compliance/)

### Information provided directly

The following information may be collected directly from users:

- Mobile phone number, required for account registration, login, account recovery, and OTP-based authentication.
- Full name, if entered by the user in the profile.
- Profile picture, if the user chooses to upload one.
- Address or service location, if provided, to help connect users with nearby caregivers or home nurses.
- Booking-related details, such as appointment requests, timing preferences, service needs, and communication submitted through the app.
- Messages, support requests, complaints, and feedback sent to customer support or grievance channels.

### Information collected automatically

When users access or use the Services, the following technical or usage information may be collected automatically:

- Device type, model, and operating system version.
- App usage information, such as screens viewed, features used, session events, crash logs, diagnostics, and performance data.
- Log information such as timestamps, app version, language, approximate technical identifiers, and error reports.
- Location-related information derived from the address entered by the user, and if enabled in a future version of the app, device-based location information subject to separate consent and permission prompts.

### Sensitive and personal data

The app may process personal data such as phone number, address, profile photo, booking details, and communications needed to deliver services. Developers publishing on Google Play are required to accurately disclose app data collection, sharing, and security practices in the Google Play Data safety section, including data handled by third-party SDKs or service providers. [developer.android](https://developer.android.com/google/play/integrity/other)

## How Information Is Used

Personal information may be used for the following purposes:

- To create, authenticate, and manage user accounts.
- To verify identity through OTP login or account verification processes.
- To match users with caregivers or home nurses and facilitate bookings.
- To display user profile information within the app where necessary for service delivery.
- To communicate about bookings, service updates, account activity, support matters, and policy changes.
- To improve app performance, fix bugs, monitor service quality, and enhance user experience.
- To maintain security, prevent misuse, detect fraud, investigate complaints, and enforce legal terms.
- To comply with legal, regulatory, law enforcement, or contractual obligations.

Where the app collects or transmits personal or sensitive user data that is not obvious from the app’s core functionality, Google Play requires a prominent in-app disclosure and affirmative consent before collection or transmission begins. [iubenda](https://www.iubenda.com/en/blog/fix-make-sure-apps-fulfill-prominent-disclosure-requirements/)

## Legal Basis and Consent

Where required by applicable law, data is processed based on user consent, performance of a service requested by the user, compliance with legal obligations, and legitimate purposes such as security, fraud prevention, and service improvement. [meity.gov](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)

Users may withdraw consent for optional information, such as profile picture or optional address fields, by updating their profile settings or contacting the Company, subject to any legal or operational limitations. [meity.gov](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)

## Permissions Requested

### Camera permission

Camera access is requested only when a user chooses to take and upload a profile picture through the app. The camera is not accessed for any unrelated purpose. [iubenda](https://www.iubenda.com/en/blog/fix-make-sure-apps-fulfill-prominent-disclosure-requirements/)

If future versions of the app request additional permissions, such as location, storage, microphone, or notifications, those permissions should be accompanied by a clear explanation in the app and this Privacy Policy should be updated accordingly. [blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-mena/product-updates/android-chrome-and-play/google-play-safety/)

## Sharing of Information

Personal information is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their independent marketing purposes. [onesignal](https://onesignal.com/blog/google-play-data-safety-compliance/)

Information may be disclosed only in the following circumstances:

- To caregivers or home nurses selected or booked by the user, limited to the information reasonably necessary to deliver the requested service.
- To service providers or vendors that support app functionality, such as OTP delivery, cloud hosting, analytics, customer support, security monitoring, or database infrastructure, subject to confidentiality and data-processing obligations. [blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-mena/product-updates/android-chrome-and-play/google-play-safety/)
- To comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, court order, or governmental request.
- To protect the rights, property, safety, users, caregivers, nurses, or the Company.
- In connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate safeguards and notice where required.

Google Play requires developers to disclose not only first-party data handling but also data collection and sharing performed through third-party libraries, SDKs, and service providers used in the app. [onesignal](https://onesignal.com/blog/google-play-data-safety-compliance/)

## Third-Party Services

The Services may rely on third-party providers for technical operations, including cloud hosting, database services, analytics, crash reporting, OTP/SMS delivery, customer communication, maps, and payment processing if enabled. These providers may process personal data on behalf of the Company only for authorized purposes related to the operation of the Services. [onesignal](https://onesignal.com/blog/google-play-data-safety-compliance/)

Examples of such providers may include:

- Cloud hosting or database vendors.
- OTP or SMS gateway providers.
- Analytics and crash reporting providers.
- Payment processors, if payments are integrated.
- Customer support or communications tools.

The actual list of providers should be reviewed against the production app stack and updated before publication so that the policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration remain accurate. [blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-mena/product-updates/android-chrome-and-play/google-play-safety/)

## Data Storage and Security

Reasonable and industry-standard administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards are used to help protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. Such measures may include restricted access controls, authentication controls, encryption in transit where applicable, secure server infrastructure, logging, and periodic security review. [developer.android](https://developer.android.com/google/play/integrity/other)

No method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed. [onesignal](https://onesignal.com/blog/google-play-data-safety-compliance/)

Personal information is retained for as long as necessary to maintain user accounts, provide booked services, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, comply with legal obligations, and support legitimate business and security needs. [meity.gov](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)

When retention is no longer necessary, data may be deleted, anonymized, or securely archived in accordance with applicable law and internal retention practices. [meity.gov](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)

## User Rights

Subject to applicable law, users may have the right to:

- Access personal information held about them.
- Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete information.
- Delete their account and request erasure of associated personal data, subject to legal or operational retention requirements.

- File a complaint or grievance regarding data handling practices.

Users can exercise these rights through in-app account settings where available, or by contacting the Company using the contact details listed below. [meity.gov](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)
